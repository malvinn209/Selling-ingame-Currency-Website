Questions

How long does delivery take?

Most orders are delivered within minutes of confirmed payment. If yours hasn't arrived after 30 minutes, message support with your order number.

Do you need my password?

No. We only need your player ID or username. Never share your password, email login or verification codes with anyone.

What if I enter the wrong ID?

Currency sent to a wrong ID usually can't be recovered or refunded, so check your ID carefully before you pay.

Which payment methods can I use?

Visa and Mastercard work in every region. QRIS, GoPay and Indomaret / Alfamart are available when the region is set to Indonesia (Rp).

How do I check my order?

Open Track order, then enter your order number and the email you used at checkout.

Can I get a refund?

See the refund policy for when an order can be refunded or re-sent.
